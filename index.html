Personal Google Workspace integration

Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is Milo's private personal-assistant integration. It connects Milo's own Google account to a locally controlled Hermes installation so requested tasks can be performed across Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets and Docs.

Access model

The integration is for its owner's personal use. Google data is accessed only after explicit OAuth consent and only to perform user-requested assistant functions. It is not sold, used for advertising or provided as a public SaaS product.
